\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Physical-AI-Fusion brings a quantitative, verification-first benchmark to fusion physics with an ICF focus. Its 699 verified problems are, to our knowledge, the first fusion-physics evaluation set in which every reference answer passes a mechanical correctness gate---schema validation, sandboxed independent recomputation, dual verification methods, and a logged promotion process---rather than an author's assurance, and in which every task is graded deterministically end-to-end. The harness makes evaluation a one-command, reproducible operation in the SWE-bench style. We hope the benchmark, the pipeline, and the public verification log together lower the cost of asking, and trusting, harder physics questions of large models.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
Models answer each verified task with a fixed instruction prompt (brief reasoning, then a single \texttt{Final answer:} line) at temperature 0.2, and responses are graded by the deterministic graders for numeric/concept/figure tasks. Derivation tasks are graded by the locked rubric judge and reported separately. We evaluate a reference-prediction control (the dataset's own answers as responses) as a grader sanity check, and frontier open-weight models served through a fixed endpoint. \emph{At submission of this preprint, the full baseline sweep is being finalized; the deterministic control and the grading pipeline are complete, and per-model tables are filled in the camera-ready update.} % BASELINE_PLACEHOLDER

\subsection{Grader sanity (control)}
The reference control scores 93/93 on deterministic tasks (100\%): numeric extraction matches every tolerance band and choice extraction matches every key. The remaining 13 derivation tasks are held out of the deterministic score by design (87.7\% of the split is deterministic-gradable). This validates the extraction rules and tolerance bands end-to-end on real response formats.
